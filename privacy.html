PRIVACY POLICY

Overview

This website, dev.nialfernandes.com, is a personal project and portfolio site. I respect your privacy and want to be upfront about the limited data this site collects.

What I collect

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. This includes things like which pages are visited, how long people stay, and what country they're browsing from. This helps me improve the site and understand what's useful.

GA4 collects data through cookies and similar technologies. It does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. IP addresses are anonymised by default in GA4.

Cookies used

Cookie Purpose Duration
_ga Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_ga_<ID> Maintains session state 2 years

This site also uses localStorage to remember your light/dark theme preference. This is not a cookie and is never sent to any server.

Third parties

Google Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy. No other third-party services collect data through this site.

Your choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using your browser's built-in cookie controls to block third-party cookies.

International visitors

This site is operated from Australia. If you're visiting from the European Union or another jurisdiction with specific data protection laws, please be aware that by using this site, your data may be processed by Google's servers in various locations. Your continued use of the site constitutes acknowledgement of this policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach me through the contact details on the main site.
