HEADLINE STRATEGY:
These rules are backed by download performance data. Follow them closely.

Aussie first: Always frame the headline through its ASX or Australian market impact, even when the underlying news is global. "Wall St falls on Trump remarks" is weak. "US slump: ASX braces for impact" is strong. An Australian angle consistently outperforms US or global framing.

Lead with the market, not the cause: Describe what happened to the market, not why. "Aussie market sinks to 2 month low" beats "RBA decision sinks ASX."

Match sentiment to market direction:
- Market up → Use momentum language (e.g. "ASX extends winning streak").
- Market down → Use dramatic language (e.g. "Trade jitters sink Aussie stocks").
- Never use neutral or bland phrasing. "A mixed session" is a download killer.

"Record" is a power word: When the data supports it (actual record highs or milestones), use "Record" prominently. Titles mentioning record highs are among the highest performers.

- Momentum: Winning, Streak, Record, Fresh, Best
- Psychology: Jitters, Fears, Nervous, Panic
- Intensity: Sinks, Slump, Soars, Plunges

LOW ENGAGEMENT WORDS (avoid these in headlines):
- Jargon: Data, CPI, Earnings, Jobs, Tariffs
- Technical: Rate, Cut, RBA, Inflation
- Passive: After, Ends, Result, Lower, Trade
