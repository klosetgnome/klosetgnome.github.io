Summary
Copy Assist — Message
Paste your rough notes or bullet points into the text box and Copy Assist will transform them into a polished, well-structured email. Choose a model from the gear icon, then hit Create. Use Recreate to generate a fresh version — previous drafts are saved in the history below.
Copy Assist — Summarise
Paste a block of text — or drop a link to any webpage — and Copy Assist will generate a concise summary. Links are fetched automatically and summarisation begins after a short countdown. Hit Recreate to try again with different wording.
Copy Assist — Review
Paste your notes or key details about a product, restaurant, or experience and Copy Assist will craft a natural-sounding review. Great for turning quick impressions into something ready to publish. Use Recreate to generate alternatives.
